# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Data Analyst Toolkit to be a welcoming, respectful community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, respectful, and constructive in discussions, reviews, and issue reports.
- Assume good intent, ask questions when something is unclear, and offer feedback about ideas rather than attacking people.
- Welcome newcomers and help keep participation accessible.
- Respect others' privacy. Do not share private data, credentials, or personal information in issues, pull requests, or examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to [sameer.kattubadi@gmail.com](mailto:sameer.kattubadi@gmail.com). Include a description of what happened and any relevant links or context. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.

The project maintainer may take appropriate action, including removing content, asking someone to change their behavior, or restricting participation. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and other community discussions about the project.
