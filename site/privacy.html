Privacy

Your data stays in the conversation.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy describes the Data Analyst Copilot plugin and this information site. The plugin is a package of written skills. It does not include an MCP server, database connection, upload service, analytics, or plugin-operated backend.

Information the plugin handles

The plugin itself does not receive or store your prompts, datasets, or analysis results. If you choose to share text, schemas, sample records, or business rules while using a skill, the ChatGPT or Codex service processes that information to provide a response. That platform’s data practices, settings, and retention controls apply to your conversation. Review the OpenAI Privacy Policy and the controls available in the product you use.

Do not share payment-card data, protected health information, government identifiers, passwords, API keys, or other authentication secrets. Remove or mask sensitive data that is not necessary for your request.

Purpose and sharing

Information you include in a conversation is used by the host platform to answer your request with the selected skill. The plugin publisher does not receive that conversation or share it with other recipients through the plugin. The plugin does not send data to third-party analysis services.

Storage and retention

The plugin publisher does not store conversation inputs or outputs. The host platform controls conversation storage and retention; consult its current privacy policy and settings for details. This static site has no forms, cookies, or analytics configured in this package. The hosting provider may process technical connection data to serve and protect the pages; the provider and its retention period depend on the hosting service selected for publication.

Your choices

  • Share only the fields and sample rows needed to answer your question; mask personal or confidential values first.
  • Use the host platform’s controls to manage or delete conversations, subject to that platform’s policy.
  • Contact the plugin publisher with privacy questions at sameer.kattubadi@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

If the plugin gains a data-processing service or this site adds analytics, forms, or other data collection, this policy must be updated before that change is released. Questions can be sent to sameer.kattubadi@gmail.com.
