Privacy

Your data stays in the conversation.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy describes Data Analyst Toolkit and its static website. The plugin is a package of written skills. It has no MCP server, database connection, upload service, analytics, or publisher-operated backend.

Information, purpose, and recipients

The plugin publisher does not collect prompts, datasets, schemas, sample records, business rules, or analysis results through the plugin. When you use a skill, information you choose to enter in your conversation is processed by the host conversation service to generate a response. Depending on the product and account you use, that service is operated by OpenAI. The plugin publisher does not receive that conversation data.

This static website has no contact form, account registration, cookies, or analytics configured in this package. When you visit it, GitHub Pages serves the site and GitHub may process technical website-usage data, such as the referring site, visit time, pages viewed, and links clicked, to operate and protect its services. GitHub is the recipient of that website request data. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for the categories of data GitHub processes and its purposes.

Do not provide payment-card data, protected health information, government identifiers, passwords, API keys, or other authentication secrets in a conversation. Remove or mask sensitive information that is not necessary for your request.

Storage and retention

The plugin publisher does not store conversation inputs or outputs. The host conversation service controls how long it retains conversation data; retention depends on the service, account type, settings, and applicable policies. GitHub controls the retention of website-usage data it processes under its privacy statement. The plugin publisher does not independently retain either category of data and cannot set or shorten those providers’ retention periods.

Your choices

  • Choose what information to include in a conversation; share only the fields and sample rows needed and mask personal or confidential values first.
  • Use your host conversation service’s available privacy and retention controls. For website requests, refer to GitHub’s privacy statement and available controls.
  • Contact the plugin publisher at sameer.kattubadi@gmail.com with questions or requests about information handled by the publisher. The publisher cannot access or delete data held by the host conversation service or GitHub.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the plugin or website’s data practices change. Questions can be sent to sameer.kattubadi@gmail.com.
